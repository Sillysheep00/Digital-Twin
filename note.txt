auto mode : systems tries to maintain the target temperature which is 22 degree celsius

night time 
the building will be allowed to cool down to 16 degree celsius
heater will turn on if it drops below 16 degree celsius
maintain temperature at 15 -16

During Day Time (Occupied)
Target: 22°C (unless customized per room)
Comfort Zone: ±2°C
Heater ON: Below 20°C
Cooling ON: Above 24°C

Eco Mode (Empty Room during Day)
If estimatedRoomOccupancy < 0.1 (Room is empty):
Comfort Zone: ±3°C
Heater ON: Below 19°C (22 - 3)
Cooling ON: Above 25°C (22 + 3)
